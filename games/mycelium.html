Mycelium

From rot, an empire.

Beneath the forest floor, a silent empire spreads. You are a fungal colony. Thread your hyphae through the dark, break the dead into nutrients, and strike bargains with the trees above.

Reach too far and a hard winter starves your edges. Grow too slow and rivals seize the richest ground. Across two years, decompose, fruit, and rule the groves before the last season turns.

2–4 players 90–130 min Age 14+
  • A living board that decays as you play
  • Bargain with trees, poison rivals, evolve to survive
  • A different forest every game, and winter always comes

In the box ~40 substrate tiles · 88 hyphae · 24 fruiting bodies
44 cards · nutrient tokens · season deck
